# Check a draft before you post it
Use the inspector's Preview and Audit tabs to see the mobile cut-off and the studio's reach checks before you post.

## Open the inspector

The inspector is the panel on the right of the Composer. It is open when the studio starts and has five tabs: **Preview**, **Audit**, **Media**, **Brand** and **Prompt**. It only appears on the Composer.

If you closed it:

1. Go to **Composer** in the rail.
2. Select the small panel icon at the top right of the Composer. It has no visible text; screen readers announce it as **Open inspector**.

You can also press `Ctrl+K` and choose **Toggle inspector**. The palette entries **Audit for reach** and **Check fold safety** do the same thing: they open or close the inspector. They do not switch to a particular tab, so if the inspector is already open they close it.

## Preview

Preview draws your post as a feed card. Choose a surface at the top:

| Tab | What you see | Caption under the post |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Mobile** | The text cut at 180 characters, then **... see more**. Select it to expand. | COLLAPSES AT 180 CHARS, or FITS ABOVE THE FOLD when the whole post fits |
| **Desktop** | The whole post, never cut | SHOWN WHOLE. THE 180 CHAR FOLD IS A MOBILE MEASUREMENT |
| **Carousel** | Always NO CAROUSEL SLIDES FOR THIS DRAFT | GENERATED BY THE CAROUSEL ENGINE, NOT THE COMPOSER |

The Preview uses no desktop cut-off figure, so Desktop shows the whole post rather than guessing. Nothing in the interface generates carousel slides, so the Carousel tab never shows any.

The Preview's character count ignores the marks that **Underline** and **Strikethrough** add, so formatted text counts the way a reader sees it.

The card shows the **Name** and **Headline** saved in **Brand Studio** under **Identity**, or the **Company** when the Headline is blank. Until you save a Name it shows grey bars instead of a name.

## Audit

The audit shows:

- **Distribution**: a score out of 100 and a label. OPTIMAL is 85 or more, MODERATE RISK is 60 to 84, HIGH PENALTY RISK is below 60. An empty draft reads EMPTY DRAFT.
- The measured rows: **Outbound links** (PRESENT or NONE), **Hashtags**, **Mentions**, **Words** and **Est. dwell** in seconds. A row turns orange when it breaks a rule.
- **Penalties**: each rule the draft breaks. With none, the panel reads NO DISTRIBUTION PENALTIES DETECTED.
- **Suggestions**: advice in a separate list. Some items have no matching penalty, such as a note that a post is brief or sits in the 45 to 90 second reading window.

## The rules and their points

The score starts at 100 and never goes below 20.

| Rule | Triggered by | Points off |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Outbound link | Any text starting `http://`, `https://` or `www.` | 35 |
| Hashtags | More than 5 | 15 |
| Dense paragraph | A block of more than 3 lines with no blank line in it | 15 |
| Dashes | Any em dash or en dash | 5 |
| Mentions | More than 3 @mentions | 10 |

The link penalty text says "-40% algorithmic reach penalty". That figure is the studio's rule of thumb. LinkedIn has not published it and the studio has not measured it. **Est. dwell** is worked out from word count at 200 words a minute. It is not a measurement of real readers.

> [!TIP]
> To clear the dash penalty, select the text and choose **Clean formatting** in the toolbar that appears over a selection. It replaces em dashes, and en dashes between words, with commas. An en dash between two numbers becomes a hyphen.

## Governance gates

Below the audit is a second check with six gates. It shows a score (the share of gates passed, as a whole number out of 100, for example 83 when five of six pass) and PASSED or BLOCKED, then either the violations or ALL SIX GATES CLEAR.

| Gate | Blocks when |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | The text has an em dash, or the words game-changer, revolutionary, synergistic or supercharge |
| 2 | The post is under 50 or over 3,000 characters |
| 3 | The first line is over 140 characters, or the first three lines together are over 210 |
| 4 | The post has no line break at all |
| 5 | The text contains `li_at=`, `session_key=`, `bot_token=` or `api_key=` followed by a value in quotes |
| 6 | Never; it always passes |

Gate 3 uses its own limits, separate from the 180-character Preview fold. Gates 2 and 3 count raw characters, so the marks that **Underline** and **Strikethrough** add do count toward them.

## What happens

Preview updates as you type. While the Audit tab is open, the audit and the gates re-run about 0.7 seconds after you stop typing. Both read the text in the editor, including changes you have not saved, and send it only to the studio's own server on this machine. The checks are fixed pattern rules. No AI model is called and nothing leaves your computer. Checking does not save the draft.

## If it does not work

| You see | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| AUDIT UNAVAILABLE | The audit request failed, usually because the studio server has stopped. See [The studio will not open or shows an error](the-studio-will-not-open.md). |
| AUDITING that never changes | The server has not answered yet. If it stays, treat it like AUDIT UNAVAILABLE. |
| No Governance gates section | That check failed on its own. The Distribution audit is unaffected. |
| A preview card with no name | No Name is saved. Add one in **Brand Studio**, press **Save**, then go back to **Composer**. The inspector reads the name again when it reopens there. |
| The **Brand** tab says EDITED IN BRAND STUDIO, WHICH IS NOT BUILT YET | That text is out of date. **Brand Studio** is in the rail. |
