# Answer the people waiting on a reply
Open **Replies** to see every comment on your posts that is waiting on a reply, grouped by post, the one that has waited longest first.

**Today** names five people under **Reply to**. Replies is the whole list. The count beside **Reply to** on Today, for example **12 COMMENTS WAITING, OPEN REPLIES**, is the number Replies lists, and clicking it opens Replies.

## What is listed

Every comment on your own posts that the studio first saw in the last 14 days, unless you choose another window, from someone you have not answered. The same rules as Today's list:

- Only comments. A reaction leaves nothing to reply to.
- Only on your own posts, which the studio knows once your post history is imported (see [Set up the studio from your own LinkedIn](set-up-the-studio-from-your-linkedin.md)).
- Not from someone whose lead status is **Outreach Sent**, **Connected** or **Meeting Booked**, because those mean you have already answered them.
- Not a comment you marked **Answered**.

Nothing appears until you have confirmed your profile in **Setup**.

## How it is ordered

Comments are grouped by the post they were left on. The post with the comment that has waited longest comes first, and inside each post the comments run oldest first. The ICP score plays no part.

Each post shows its three longest-waiting comments, so one busy post cannot fill the screen and hide the others. **Show all** and the count, for example **Show all 106 on this post**, opens that post with every comment waiting on it; **All posts** goes back.

## The time beside a comment

Each comment says **SEEN BY THE STUDIO** and how long ago, for example **SEEN BY THE STUDIO 3 DAYS AGO**. That is when the bridge first read the comment on your post's page, not when the person wrote it: LinkedIn's own time is not captured. Opening the post again does not change it.

## Answered is your own mark

The studio cannot read from LinkedIn whether you replied, so it does not guess. When you have answered a comment, click **Answered** beside it. The comment leaves the list, and a line at the top says whose comment you marked, with **Undo**. Marking also takes the comment off Today's list; a person stays there while another comment of theirs is waiting.

**Marked answered** at the top of the screen lists the comments you marked in the window, with when you marked each one, and **Waiting** goes back to the list. **Not answered** beside a comment takes its mark back, and the comment is waiting again.

If you forget a person in **Leads**, their marks go with them.

## The figures at the top

| Figure | What it counts |
| --- | --- |
| Comments waiting | The comments listed, across every page, in the window you chose |
| People waiting | How many people left them |
| Posts they are on | How many of your posts they were left on |
| Marked answered, last 7 days | How many comments you marked answered in the last 7 days, whenever the comment was seen |

When nobody is waiting, the screen says so and names how many you marked answered in the last 7 days.

## Other controls

- **Window** chooses the last 7, 14 or 30 days. Today always uses 14.
- **Open the post** opens the post in the bridge browser, where you can reply. A post the studio has no LinkedIn address for has no such button.
- A person's name opens their dossier in **Leads**.
- **Show more posts** reads the next eight posts, and on an opened post **Show more on this post** reads its next 50 comments; the line beside it says how many are shown of how many.

Replies reads its list again within about a second of the bridge keeping a new comment on one of your posts, and of a comment being marked or unmarked in another studio window; every minute while the window is in view, for anything nothing announced; and when you come back to it. **Today**'s list and its count follow a mark the same way.
