# See what worked, and where your attention goes

Use **Posts** to see which of your posts did better than your usual and who each one brought, and **Outbound** to see whose content you engage with and whether they engage back.

Both screens are built from your own imported history. Import your posts, comments and reactions from **Setup** first; see [Set up the studio from your own LinkedIn](set-up-the-studio-from-your-linkedin.md).

## Posts

The line under the title gives your usual: the median reactions, comments and impressions of your posts that are at least a week old.

Each post shows its date, opening, and the reactions, comments and impressions last read on it. For a post at least a week old, each figure is followed by how it compares with your usual, for example "55 REACTIONS (1.8x)". Green is at least 1.2 times your usual, orange is 0.8 times or less. A post under a week old is marked **STILL GROWING** and not compared, because its numbers are still arriving.

The two week-old posts furthest above your usual reactions are marked **WORTH REPURPOSING**: their form has already worked for your audience.

Click a post to see the people the studio captured engaging with it. Click a name to open their dossier in **Leads**. **CONVERSATIONS** counts the people from that post whose lead status you moved to **Connected** or **Meeting Booked**. If a post shows nobody, open it with **Open the post**; its commenters are read when it opens in the bridge browser.

## Outbound

The line under the title counts your reactions and comments on other people's posts, and how many people and pages they were on.

| Section | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Engaged back** | People you engage with who have also engaged with one of your posts |
| **One-way** | People and pages you have engaged with at least three times who have not engaged with your posts |
| **Most engaged with** | Everyone you engage with, most first, with your reactions, your comments, and how often they appeared on your posts |

**One-way** is not shown until the studio has read who engaged with at least half of your posts. Until then it says how many it has read. Opening your posts in the bridge browser, for example from **Posts**, is what reads them.

> [!NOTE]
> "Engaged back" is matched by display name. A reaction records the author's name, not their profile, so two people with the same name would be counted together.

## Lead history

In a lead's dossier in **Leads**, each change you make under **Pipeline** is now kept, and listed under the buttons with its date. That history is what lets **Posts** count conversations.
