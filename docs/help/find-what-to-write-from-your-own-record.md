# Find what to write from your own record

Open **From your record** for five cards a day, drawn from your own posts, your comments and what you asked for, each with the rows it came from and the rule that chose it.

## Where it is

**From your record** is in the rail's **Now** group, after **Today** and **Replies**, and once today's cards are built, **Today** lists them, one line each, with a link here. The buttons at the top right switch between its four views: **Today's cards**, **Kept**, **What the studio knows** and **Did these help?**

It needs no AI provider and, without one, sends nothing anywhere. Import your LinkedIn archive in **Setup** and it has cards from the first day. See [Set up the studio from your LinkedIn](set-up-the-studio-from-your-linkedin.md).

## When the cards are made

The cards are made once a day, the first time the studio runs that day: a minute after it starts, then checked every hour while it runs, so a studio left running past midnight makes the new day's cards within the hour. The day is your computer's. Opening the section reads the cards already made rather than making them, so it opens at once.

The line under the title says when they were made, for example COMPUTED TODAY AT 09:14. Before today's cards exist it shows the last ones and says so: COMPUTED YESTERDAY AT 21:02; TODAY'S RUN HAS NOT HAPPENED YET.

When nothing the cards are made from has changed since the last day (your posts and their readings, your comments and reactions, your asks and marks, your archive, the library and your provider), the same cards stay and a line says "Nothing new since" that day. Only the second card, which follows the date, and the Monday line are drawn again.

- **Suggest new** draws today's cards again, once a day. Your marks on the earlier cards still count, and a card you kept stays in **Kept**.
- **Day**, once there is more than one, shows the cards of each of the last 14 days that had them, as they were. On an earlier day you can keep a card or open its post, nothing else.
- Each Monday, **Your week** above the cards counts what the studio recorded in the seven days before: posts published, comments and reactions you gave, pages the bridge read and replies answered. A second line counts the section's own week: how many of last week's cards were shown, and how many you kept, skipped, hid, drafted from and posted; a week with no card shown says so instead. Counts only, compared with nothing. A count the studio does not record reads "not measured", never 0.

## The five cards

| Card | What it shows | How it is chosen |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Working for you**: What worked | The post of yours that did best against your usual reactions, with its figures and the page and date they were read from | Settled posts only, against your own median, ranked the way **Posts** ranks them. Until three of your posts have settled with figures, it says how many have, for example "Not enough settled posts yet: 1 of 3." |
| **Working for you**: On this day, Opener to reuse, or An article of yours | A post of yours from this date in an earlier year; when there is none, the first line of one of your posts, or as often an article you published, from your archive, whose title goes under one of your recurring words | The opener comes from the top quarter of your ranked posts once eight are ranked, otherwise from all of them, each with the chance of its topic's share of your posts. A line or article once shown is held back 7 days, then 14, then 28, and its chance halves each time. |
| **You asked** | Something you asked for, with up to three of your own posts that share its words, and the section of the studio's playbook that shares at least half of them, with **Open it in Docs** | Your asks in turn. With nothing asked yet, one of the words that recur across your posts. |
| **You engaged with** | A comment you left on someone else's post: your own words, the date and a link to the post. Or a poll you voted in, or a company or member you follow, when it goes under one of your recurring words; or a post you reacted to, reposted or saved, as a link and a date | A comment comes up twice as often as each of the other three kinds. The other person's words are never put on a card: a poll shows its topic and its link, never its options, and a follow shows the name and the date your archive holds, with nothing read about the account. |
| The fifth card | One idea from your own AI provider, or an entry of the Inox library that suits one of your topics | See the two sections below. Until either is set up, the card says how to fill it. |

Every card shows, beside its title, how it was arrived at (for example "measured" or "resurfaced, not measured"), your own words in quotation marks where it has any, figures only where a page was read, and a line that starts "Because". **Why this?** opens the whole rule, the rows each figure was read from, and the card's label: MEASURED (read from a row, with when), DERIVED (worked out from measured rows by the stated rule), TYPED (sorted by a rule such as shared words, with nothing measured behind it), SUGGESTED (written by your model, then checked) or CURATED (from the Inox library). Before a card is shown, every row it cites is looked up; a card whose row is gone is dropped and counted under the title, never shown.

The cards that are drawn keep to different topics. Each takes one of your recurring topics that no earlier card of the day shows, when it has one to take, so five cards are not five takes on one theme; a repeat comes only when everything a slot could show goes under a topic already there. **What worked** and **On this day** follow your record and are never changed for it, and **Why this?** says the rule.

A card that cannot be filled yet is dashed and reads NOT YET, with a sentence saying what is missing and what fills it. It is never padded with another card.

## Act on a card

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Keep** | Puts the card in **Kept**. A drawn card (an opener, an ask, a recurring word, something you engaged with, a library entry) becomes twice as likely to come back. Press **Kept** to undo it |
| **Skip** | A drawn card stays away 14 days, then comes back half as often. **What worked** steps aside for 14 days for the next post in the same ranking, and **On this day** for another post of the date or an opener |
| **Hide** | The card is not shown again: **What worked** gives way to the next post in the same ranking, **On this day** to another post of the date or an opener. **Unhide all** on **What the studio knows** undoes every hide |
| **More of this** | Keeps the card's topic as an ask: the recurring word of yours it goes under, or its own words when it goes under none |
| **Draft from this** | Opens the **Composer** on a new draft holding the card's words, and records that you drafted from it. When that draft is published or sent, the card is marked as posted |
| **Open on LinkedIn** | Opens the post in the bridge browser |

Marks change which cards come back, from the next day's cards. They never change a figure.

## Ask for something

An ask is kept only when you do one of these on purpose:

- **Ask for something to write about**, under today's cards, then **Remember this ask**.
- The three optional questions under **What you want from your record (optional)**, in **Setup** and on **What the studio knows**. What you want to write about is matched to your posts like any ask. Who you write for, and what the studio should never suggest, are told only to your own model, and only when its stage is on. Each can be skipped.
- In the Composer's **Prompt** tab, an instruction you run with **Remember this ask for From your record** ticked. See [Run an instruction on your draft](run-an-instruction-on-your-draft.md).
- **More of this** on a card.
- An `Idea: your text` message sent to your Telegram bot, which becomes an ask when the next day's cards are made. See [Send a post idea from Telegram](send-a-post-idea-from-telegram.md).
- A search from your archive's `SearchQueries.csv`, only if you ticked that file in **Setup** and only when it shares one of your recurring topic words. These are never sent to your model.

An ask is matched to your posts from the next day's cards.

## Kept

Every card you kept, newest first: your own posts, openers, articles, asks, comments, votes, follows and library entries. **Keep** is the one way in, and a card you hid is left out.

## What the studio knows

Everything the section holds about you, on one page, each part changeable:

- **Your asks**: each with where it came from and when. **Edit**, **Retire** (kept but no longer used; **Use again** brings it back) or **Remove for good**.
- The three optional questions, as in **Setup**.
- **Your marks**: how many cards you kept, skipped, hid, drafted from and posted, the cards you hid, and **Unhide all**.
- **From your LinkedIn archive**: how many saved posts, follows, poll votes, articles, reposts and searches your archive brought.
- **How long the daily cards are kept**: 90 days, 365 days (the default) or for good. A day that a mark or a draft refers to is never removed. Nothing removes an ask or a mark on its own: an ask goes with **Remove for good**, a keep when you press **Kept**, a hide with **Unhide all**.
- **Your own model** and **The Inox library**, below.
- **The daily batch**: when the cards were last made. **Health** says the same, beside the model stage's spend and the library's signature.

Deleting everything the studio holds about you, under **Your profile** in **Setup**, also removes your asks, the daily cards, your marks and your notes.

## One idea a day from your own model

With an AI provider connected (see [Connect your own AI provider](connect-your-own-ai-provider.md)), the fifth card holds one idea a day from it. It is asked the first time you open **From your record** each day, as its own request: never by the daily run and never while the section is closed. The other cards are on screen first; the idea arrives in the fifth when your provider answers. On a day when nothing changed, yesterday's idea stands and your provider is not asked again.

It is sent the first 300 characters of up to ten of your own posts, each with its topic and the studio's word for how it did against your usual, never a figure; up to twelve of your latest asks, never a search from your archive; who you write for and what to avoid, if you answered those questions; your writing habits in words; and who you are, from the profile you confirmed. Inside your own text, the name of anybody the studio has a record of, an @mention, an email address, a phone number and a link are replaced before it leaves. It is never sent another person's words, your connections or a score. **See what would be sent**, under **Your own model** on **What the studio knows**, shows the exact text.

What comes back is checked before it is shown. An idea is dropped, not repaired, when it cites a post or an ask that was not sent, or a row that is gone, quotes words that are not in your post, carries a number, names a person, or runs past 160 characters. The line under the title says how many came back and why any were dropped. One idea that passes is shown, labelled with your provider; the studio checks it for numbers and names, not for whether it is good.

- One call a day, or two if the first failed, with up to 1,200 tokens back.
- A daily ceiling in US dollars, $0.10 unless you change it. The cost is estimated before the call from your provider's prices on a stated date; a call that could go over what is left is not made, and the line under the title says so. A model that is not in the studio's price table is estimated at its provider's dearest listed price. A provider with no listed price, such as Groq, is not called, because the studio could not keep it under your ceiling. A model on this computer costs nothing.
- Beside the switch, the studio says what your provider says it does with what it is sent, and the date that was read, or that it has not read your provider's terms.
- The switch **Ask ... for one idea a day, the first time I open this section** turns it off: nothing is sent.
- During the beta every studio has it. Once the beta ends it is part of the Creator licence. See [Keep each creator separate, and enter a licence](keep-each-creator-separate-and-enter-a-licence.md).

## The Inox library

The library is forms, patterns and examples the Inox Hydra team wrote, each tagged with the topics it suits: no post copied from LinkedIn, no person's name, no figure. When your own model's idea is not there, an entry that suits one of your recurring topics fills the fifth card, labelled curated. The matching happens on this computer.

- **Fetch the Inox library**, on **What the studio knows**, asks one fixed address for one file. The request carries that address, a User-Agent naming the library channel and the tag of the copy you already hold, and nothing of yours: no post, ask, mark, name or setting.
- **Import a library file** takes the same file from disk, for a computer that never connects.
- Every file is checked against the library key that ships with the studio before anything in it is kept. A file whose signature does not match is refused and changes nothing.

See [What leaves this machine, and when](what-leaves-this-machine.md) for the switches that stop either request.

## Did these help?

- **What you kept, drafted from and posted**: for each kind of card, and again for each label, how many were shown, how many you kept of those you kept or skipped, drafted from, and posted. Under 5, the counts alone; from 5 to 19, a ~ before them; from 20, the rate beside the counts. These are your judgements of the cards, not what worked.
- **Posts from cards, against your others**: your posts of the last 90 days that came from a card, against your other posts of the same 90 days, read at about two days old, as a ratio of the two medians with both counts. It says nothing until 8 posts a side have a reading, or while those posts are too few to show a difference smaller than a doubling, and says so.
- **Month by month**: a sentence for each of the last three months.
- **Did they help this month? In your own words**: a note you keep here, sent nowhere.

## If it does not work

| What you see | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| NOTHING COMPUTED YET | The studio has not made a batch yet. The first is made a minute after the studio starts; come back then. |
| TODAY'S RUN HAS NOT HAPPENED YET | The cards shown are from the day named. Today's come at the next hourly check while the studio runs. |
| "Not enough settled posts yet: 1 of 3." | Fewer than three of your posts are old enough and have figures. Press **Import your history** in **Setup**; the card fills as your posts settle. |
| "No readings yet" | The studio holds your posts but no figures for them. Press **Import your history** in **Setup**. |
| The fifth card says it waits for your provider or the library | Connect a provider in the Composer's **Prompt** tab, or fetch the Inox library on **What the studio knows**. |
| "Not sent: the call could cost up to ..." | The estimate is over what is left of your daily ceiling. Raise the ceiling on **What the studio knows**, or choose a cheaper model. |
| "Not sent: no price is listed for ..." | The studio cannot keep that provider under your ceiling, so it does not call it. |
| "Your own model's ideas are part of the Creator licence." | Enter a Creator licence in **Brand Studio**. |
| "The library file was not found at its address. It may not be published yet." | There is no library at the address yet. Nothing was changed. |
| "The file's signature does not match any library key this studio holds" | The file was not signed by Inox Hydra, or was changed after it was signed. Nothing was kept. |
| A card failed a check and is not shown | A row it cited was not found, or your model's idea failed one of the checks above. Nothing is put in its place. |
