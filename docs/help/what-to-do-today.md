# What to do today

Open **Today** to see who is waiting on a reply, how your latest post is doing against your usual, and the one LinkedIn page most worth opening now.

Once you have confirmed your profile in **Setup**, the studio opens on **Today**. It refreshes itself every 30 seconds while it is open, so captures from the bridge browser appear without reloading.

## The line under the title

It says whether the bridge browser has been heard from, when anything was last captured, and how many parts of the capture have stopped working. If a part has stopped, **Setup** shows which one under **CAPTURE HEALTH**.

## One thing to open

A single suggestion, with its reason. The studio picks it by a fixed order, because each item blocks the ones after it:

| Suggestion | When |
| --- | --- |
| **Go to Setup** | The studio does not know which account is yours, a part of the capture has stopped working, or your post history has not been imported |
| Open LinkedIn | The bridge browser has not been heard from recently |
| Your latest post's analytics page | Your latest post is under three days old and its figures were not read in the last 12 hours |
| Your latest post | Otherwise, because opening it picks up anyone who has commented since |

**Open it in the bridge browser** opens the page in the bridge browser, where the extension reads it.

## Reply to

Up to five people who engaged with your own posts in the last two weeks and have not heard from you. Someone whose lead status is **Outreach Sent**, **Connected** or **Meeting Booked** is left off.

They are ordered by what they did: people who engaged with more than one of your posts first, then a comment ahead of a reaction, then the most recent. The ICP score plays no part. Click a person to open their dossier in **Leads**.

## Your latest post

The opening of your most recent post, its age, and the reactions and comments last read on it.

When it can be done fairly, the studio compares it with your usual:

- Both are read at about two days old, between 36 and 72 hours. A post read at six hours and one read at six weeks say nothing about each other.
- Your usual is the median of your earlier posts read at that age, up to your last 20.
- At least three earlier posts must have a reading at that age.

The figure reads, for example, "70 vs 35 (2.0x) median of 4". Green is at least 1.2 times your usual, orange is 0.8 times or less.

When the comparison cannot be made, the screen says why instead of showing a number: the post is too new, it was not read at about two days old, or too few earlier posts were.

### Where the figures come from

Each time your posts are imported or re-read, the counts LinkedIn shows on them are kept as a reading, with the post's age at that moment. At most one reading per post per hour is kept.

**Open its analytics** opens the post's own analytics page, which only you can see. The extension reads impressions, members reached, reactions, comments, reposts, saves and sends from it, for your own recorded posts only.

## Queue

The next post scheduled to go out, and the next open slot in your weekly posting schedule.
