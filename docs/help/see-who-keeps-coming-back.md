# See who keeps coming back
Open **Audience** to see, among the people the bridge has seen, who keeps engaging with your posts, who has gone quiet, who is new each week, and who is already a connection.

**Leads** is one person at a time and your next step with them. Audience is the whole set. Nothing on it is ordered by the ICP score: the lists are ordered by how many of your posts a person engaged with, then by how recently the studio saw them do it.

## What it rests on

The bridge reads who engaged with a post only when you open that post in the bridge browser. So every figure here is about the posts whose engagers were read, not every post you have written, and each list says so. "Engaged with three of your posts" means three of the posts whose engagers were read.

A time here is when the bridge first read the engagement, not when the person reacted or commented: LinkedIn's own time is not captured. Opening a post again does not change it.

## The four figures

| Figure | What it counts |
| --- | --- |
| Repeat engagers | People who engaged with two or more of your posts, among the posts whose engagers were read. The label says how many posts that is |
| Gone quiet | Repeat engagers not seen on your last five posts whose engagers were read. **not judged yet** until five posts have been read |
| New people this week, partial | People the studio first saw engage with one of your posts this week. **nothing read yet** when the bridge has read no engagement this week |
| Connections | Your first-degree connections, from LinkedIn's data archive, as of the day you imported it. **no archive imported** until you have |

## Keep coming back

The repeat engagers, the most posts first, then the most recent. Each row shows how many of your posts they engaged with and when the studio last saw them engage. The first ten are shown; **Show** lists up to 25, and the line under the list says how many there are in all. A name opens the person in **Leads**.

## Gone quiet

People who engaged with two or more of your posts and are not seen on any of your last five posts whose engagers were read. Someone who reacted to an older post since is still listed: the list is about those five posts. Until the engagers of five of your posts have been read, the list says it has not judged, because "not seen" would only mean "not looked".

This is not the cleanup review in **Storage and privacy**, which lists people untouched for 24 months so you can choose to remove them. See [Choose what the studio keeps](choose-what-the-studio-keeps.md).

## New people by week

People counted in the week the studio first saw them engage with one of your posts, Monday to Sunday, for the last twelve weeks, newest first. It is partial: someone who engaged with a post whose engagers were never read is not counted. A week in which the bridge read no engagement at all says **nothing read** rather than 0.

## Connections

LinkedIn's data archive lists your connections, with the date you connected. Setup imports it; see [Set up the studio from your own LinkedIn](set-up-the-studio-from-your-linkedin.md). The list is as of that import, and the title says the date.

Search by name, company or role. A connection who is also one of your leads has **LEAD** beside them, which opens them in **Leads**. The line above the search says how many of your leads are connections.

The studio matches a lead to a connection by their profile address, written the same way on both sides first (capitals, a trailing slash and the way accented letters are written are set aside). One case cannot be matched: when someone reacts to your post, LinkedIn often links them by a member number rather than by their name, and the archive's connections are listed by name. The line above the search says how many people the bridge knows only by a member number.

## Your pipeline and companies

**Your pipeline and companies, as counts**, at the foot of the screen, opens two lists:

- **Stages you set in Leads**: how many people are at each stage, such as **New Lead**, **Outreach Sent**, **Connected** and **Meeting Booked**. These are the stages you chose, nothing worked out for you.
- **Companies, as headlines name them**: the companies named most often in people's headlines, and how many people name none.

No score, tier or rate is shown here.

## In the dossier

A person's dossier in **Leads** shows **First seen**, **Last seen** and **Times seen**: the first and last time the bridge read an engagement of theirs, and how many readings there were, the same comment read again included. A person added by hand, never seen by the bridge, shows when they were added instead.

When you have imported your archive, the dossier also says "Connected since" and the date, as of the archive's import, or that they are not among your connections, or that it cannot tell because the bridge knows them only by a member number. See [Review leads and draft a message](review-leads-and-draft-a-message.md).
