## Found while building, for other tracks and the coordinator

- `results.figures` takes a post's newest reading. An export reading of a top post (impressions and engagements, reactions NULL) dated at the range's end can become a post's newest reading, and the post's figures then show reactions "not measured" where an older page reading had them. Every reading is kept; tracks 4 and 5 own how a post's readings are shown and derived.

- The export follows the page reader in keeping a day's engagements in `analytics_daily.reactions` (the help's "Reactions: The engagements card"). If a later track gives daily engagements their own column, the export's days move with the page's.
- `tests/test_egress_surface.py` now lists `schemas.openxmlformats.org` among the names that are not egress: it is the XML namespace an .xlsx is written in, matched by name, never contacted.
- The policy's "Last updated" line and version are unchanged (25 October 2026, 2.7.0); they change at the release. `release/privacy.html` was rebuilt after each policy edit; gh-pages was not touched.
- Deleting everything in Setup now also removes the phone's desk requests and the export's record of its imports; Setup's list of what goes, which is not this track's text, does not name them.
