## Notes

**Two identities, one control.** The byline (`settings.creator_profile`) and the confirmed profile (`creator_identity`) stay two records, because they answer two questions: what LinkedIn shows, and how the creator signs images. Identity sets them side by side, and "Use what LinkedIn shows" appears only when they differ.

**One session control.** Setup said "stored" when an `li_at` row existed; Brand Studio said PRESENT when both tokens decrypt. `/api/auth/status` answers both now: `stored` (anything on disk, deletable) and `client_session` (usable), with `saved_at` and, for a session the extension's Sync captured, the account.

**Due lists stay one list.** The dispatch rule was first added in the Queue's route; Today and the Queue then answered with different lists, which `tests/test_qc3_t5_queue_due.py` caught. It is in `today.due_posts` now.

**What the demo seeds.** The suite runs with `INOX_DEMO_DATA=1`. The demo queue sits at the next open slots, so a test counting queued posts in the coming days would see two; the queue, today and post modules were run on the change before it was committed, and none did.
