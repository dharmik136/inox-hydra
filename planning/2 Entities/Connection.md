# Connection

`connections` (migration 18), from [[LinkedIn Data Archive Import]]: profile URL, first and last name, company, position, connected on. Never an email address, though the archive carries some.

**Not yet used** by the lead dossier or outbound matching.

It answers questions the product cannot today: is this [[Lead]] already connected, since when, and which connections never engage. It also gives [[Outbound Engagement]] someone to match against besides a display name.
