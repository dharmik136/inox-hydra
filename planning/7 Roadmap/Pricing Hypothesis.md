# Pricing Hypothesis

Reference points (late 2026, from public pricing pages and roundups; check again before publishing):
- Taplio: Starter $39, Growth $69, Pro $199 per month; about 25% less billed annually.
- AuthoredUp: about $19.95 per month for an individual.
- Shield: about $25 per month in several roundups; one source says it has shut down.

**Hypothesis to test with design partners:**
- Creator: $12 per month billed annually ($144 per year), or $249 lifetime with a year of updates.
- Agency: $39 per month for up to 5 profiles.
- Bring-your-own AI key means no model cost to carry, which is what makes these prices possible.

The pitch is not "cheaper Taplio". It is "your LinkedIn record, on your machine, and a reason to reply within the hour".
