# Roadmap

From a working personal tool to a product strangers pay for. Dates assume one builder plus agents, starting Monday 2026-10-19.

| Phase | Window | Exit criterion |
|---|---|---|
| [[Phase 0 - Stabilise and Tell the Truth]] | Oct 19 to Oct 25 | Suite green in CI on three platforms with browser tests running; README claims all true; one product name; v2.6.0 tagged with a changelog |
| [[Phase 1 - Own Data Complete]] | Oct 26 to Nov 15 | Everything the helper captures is exportable (JSON, CSV, Obsidian); archive import works on a real archive; profile reaches the AI; account switching isolates data |
| [[Phase 2 - Commercial Plumbing]] | Nov 16 to Dec 6 | A stranger can pay, receive a licence, install a signed build and the store-listed extension, and activate offline |
| [[Phase 3 - Design Partner Beta]] | Dec 7 to Jan 10 | 15 creators on clean machines for three weeks; top 20 issues fixed; retention and the "meeting from a comment" story measured by interview, not telemetry |
| [[Phase 4 - Launch]] | Jan 11 to Jan 31, 2027 | Public store listing, pricing page, launch posts written in the studio itself |
