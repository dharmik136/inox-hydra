# Competitive Landscape

| Product | What it is | Where Inox Hydra differs |
|---|---|---|
| Taplio | Cloud suite: AI writing, scheduling, CRM, analytics | Local; never messages or comments for you, and posts only the one you press Send to LinkedIn on; your data never leaves |
| AuthoredUp | Composer and analytics extension, no AI generation | Leads, attribution, Today, AI with your own key |
| Shield | Deep analytics. Wound down May 2026: "Both Google and LinkedIn have made it clear that we cannot continue operating Shield as it was built", its notice to users as AuthoredUp quotes it (https://authoredup.com/blog/shield-analytics-winding-down); pulled from the Chrome Web Store; its method was the login cookie reused on Shield's servers for background sync [reported] | Analytics tied to who each post brought, read from pages you open, held on your own disk, never on a server |
| Kleo | AI writer and swipe file. Its Chrome extension shut down in June 2025 after overlaying and reading LinkedIn's pages; its co-founder wrote that "LinkedIn told us to" (https://www.linkedin.com/posts/jakezward_i-didnt-want-to-post-this-but-i-have-to-activity-7341796212687175681-JjZx, 2025-06-20 by its activity id); relaunched as a paid web app | Grounded in your own posts and audience, with provenance; nothing in LinkedIn's pages but one invisible attribute on feed cards, and the Stop banner during an import |
| Supergrow and similar | AI writers on LinkedIn's official API (vendor claim), swipe files | Grounded in your own posts and audience, with provenance |

**What sets it apart:** the evidence discipline (nothing invented on screen), the reply habit ([[Reply Queue]]), and owning your record ([[Obsidian Vault Export]], [[LinkedIn Data Archive Import]], and from cycle 4 LinkedIn's own analytics export as the sanctioned source of exact figures).

**Standing with LinkedIn's terms (corrected cycle 4):** tolerated in practice, not permitted in text. The default install reads only pages you open, in the foreground, into your own disk; by LinkedIn's wording that still "copies" data, and the two tools above that were stopped crossed lines this one does not (cookie reuse on vendor servers; overlays at scale). See [[Risk - LinkedIn terms and detection]].
