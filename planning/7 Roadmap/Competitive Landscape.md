# Competitive Landscape

| Product | What it is | Where Inox Hydra differs |
|---|---|---|
| Taplio | Cloud suite: AI writing, scheduling, CRM, analytics | Local; never messages or comments for you, and posts only the one you press Send to LinkedIn on; your data never leaves |
| AuthoredUp | Composer and analytics extension, no AI generation | Leads, attribution, Today, AI with your own key |
| Shield | Deep analytics (status uncertain) | Analytics tied to who each post brought |
| Supergrow, Kleo and similar | AI writers, swipe files | Grounded in your own posts and audience, with provenance |

**Defensible:** the evidence discipline (nothing invented on screen), the reply habit ([[Reply Queue]]), and owning your record ([[Obsidian Vault Export]], [[LinkedIn Data Archive Import]]).
