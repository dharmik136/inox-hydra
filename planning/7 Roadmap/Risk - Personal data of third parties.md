# Risk - Personal data of third parties

Engagers' names, headlines and comments are personal data under GDPR, CCPA and India's DPDP Act, even stored locally by the creator. The vendor does not hold the data, which helps, but the product should help the creator act responsibly.

**Already:** local storage, a purge path, 90-day archiving of strangers' leads.

**To add:** a privacy policy; a one-click "forget this person"; cloud model calls that send aggregates rather than names unless the creator turns that on ([[Data Rules for AI]], [[Audience Pack]]); export that includes everything so access requests can be answered ([[Gap - Export omits your own data]]).
