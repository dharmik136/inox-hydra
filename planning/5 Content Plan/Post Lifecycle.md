# Post Lifecycle

idea, draft, queued, handed to LinkedIn, published (seen by a reader), bound ([[Post Permalink Binder]]), then measured at about 1 hour, 24 hours, 48 hours and 7 days ([[Post Metric Observation]]).

A post is "published" in the studio only once a reader has seen it on LinkedIn. Scheduling it is not publishing it.
