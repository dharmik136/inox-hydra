# Content Plan Overview

What the LinkedIn helper hands us, and what we turn it into.

**The raw material** (all of it the creator's own, read from pages they opened):
1. Who they are: [[Creator Identity]], [[Creator Positions and Skills]].
2. What they published and how each post did over time: [[Own Post]], [[Post Metric Observation]].
3. Who answered: [[Lead]], [[Lead Interaction]].
4. Who they answer: [[Outbound Engagement]].
5. How the account moves: [[Analytics Daily]].
6. What they are thinking about next: [[Draft]], [[Telegram Ingress]], their own notes through [[MCP Grounding]].

**What it becomes, for the AI:** five context packs, each with a budget and a rule about what may leave the machine:
- [[Voice Pack]]: sound like this person.
- [[Audience Pack]]: write for the people who actually answer.
- [[Evidence Pack]]: lean on what has worked, only where the sample says so.
- [[Engagement Pack]]: who to reply to now.
- [[Outbound Pack]]: whose conversations to join.

**What it becomes, for the creator:** [[Post Drafts and Hooks]], [[Reply Queue]], [[Lead Dossier and DM]], [[Patterns and Attribution]], and an [[Obsidian Vault Export]] they can think in.

**The loops it drives:** [[Idea Pipeline]], [[Post Lifecycle]], [[Engagement Loop]].

**The rules:** [[Data Rules for AI]].
