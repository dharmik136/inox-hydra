# Content Pillars

Today topics are a regex's guess ([[Topic]]). The creator should name three to five pillars once, and the studio should tag posts against them (deterministically, with a model only as a suggestion the creator confirms).

Then [[Patterns and Attribution]] can say "posts in pillar X bring qualified leads at twice your usual rate", and the [[Queue]] can show pillar balance across the month.
